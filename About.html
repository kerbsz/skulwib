OUR PRINCIPAL

FLAG CEREMONY

Every Monday, San Jose Litex Senior High School holds a flag ceremony to start the week with a sense of unity, patriotism, and community. This ceremony is a significant tradition where students, faculty, and staff come together to show respect for the nation and reinforce the values of the school.


HISTORY

"San Jose Litex Senior High School was established in 2016 with the vision to provide a nurturing and stimulating environment for students to excel academically, personally, and socially. Since its inception, our school has been a beacon of learning, catering to young minds from diverse backgrounds, fostering a sense of unity and understanding among our students."




ACESS

San Jose Litex Senior High School values diversity in background, experience, interests, and perspectives. We seek to cultivate a campus environment where all students have the opportunity to learn from each other’s experiences and to think critically about their own views and preconceptions. The school provides all students with the opportunity and tools to build community and connection across racial, socioeconomic, geographical, and political lines that often present barriers to greater understanding, mutual respect, and authentic friendship.

San Jose Litex Senior High School nurtures well-rounded individuals through a comprehensive curriculum that integrates academic rigor with character development and extracurricular activities. We believe in fostering a supportive learning environment where students can explore their passions, develop their talents, and build lasting relationships. Opportunities to foster social responsibility and an abiding appreciation for ethics are integral to the San Jose Litex Senior High School educational experience.
